# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Inferred from the explicit project brief: men shopping for contemporary, versatile menswear in Nigeria, primarily seeking well-considered everyday pieces.

## Product Purpose

SIMPLECOTT is an ecommerce storefront for discovering and purchasing contemporary menswear. Success means visitors can confidently find relevant pieces, understand product details, and complete a clear shopping flow.

## Positioning

Inferred from the explicit project brief: modern menswear built around simplicity, versatility, and understated confidence rather than trend-led excess.

## Operating Context

Visitors browse editorial collections and clothing categories, compare products, select sizes and colors, add items to a persistent cart, and move through checkout.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The requested storefront includes homepage, shop, product detail, collections, about, cart, and checkout surfaces, with responsive navigation and functional search, filtering, sorting, wishlist, size/color selection, quantity controls, newsletter feedback, and cart persistence. This delivery is a frontend prototype only: do not imply real inventory, order processing, payment processing, or a production backend. Use Nigerian naira and the prices explicitly supplied in the brief.

## Brand Commitments

SIMPLECOTT. The requested brand line is “Simple. Sharp. Confident.” The brief commits to minimalist, masculine, editorial menswear, a neutral black/off-white/cream/charcoal/gray palette with subtle earth accents, bold modern sans typography, whitespace, asymmetry, and immersive fashion imagery.

## Evidence on Hand

The product catalog names and prices in the project brief are approved prototype content. No supplied logo, product photography, customer reviews, testimonials, or business/fulfillment evidence is present; do not present invented proof as real. External fashion imagery is illustrative.

## Product Principles

- Make product discovery and purchase paths clear across devices.
- Favor versatile everyday clothing and useful product detail.
- Keep all shopping controls meaningfully interactive.
- Be explicit that checkout is a prototype, not a real transaction.
